# Pawsome Threads GA4 Context

## Property

- Property name: Pawsome Threads - GA4
- Website domain: pawsomethreads.com

## Business

- Business type: e-commerce (pet apparel)
- What the organization does: Designs and sells premium clothing and accessories for dogs, including seasonal collections, breed-specific sizing, and custom embroidery options.

## Website Goals

- Drive online purchases through the product catalog
- Build email list via discount-on-first-order signup
- Increase repeat purchases through style guides and seasonal lookbooks
- Grow social proof with customer photo gallery submissions

## Meaningful Actions

- Purchases (add to cart, checkout initiated, purchase completed)
- Email signups (first-order discount popup)
- Size guide usage (indicates high purchase intent)
- Customer photo submissions
- Gift card purchases

## Known Data Caveats

- Instagram shopping links sometimes appear as Direct instead of Organic Social due to in-app browser behavior.
- Holiday sale periods (Black Friday, Christmas) create large traffic spikes that can distort month-over-month comparisons.
- The custom embroidery configurator fires multiple page_view events per session, which may inflate page view counts for `/custom-embroidery`.

## Interpretation Notes

- Product pages and collection pages are the highest-value content. Category pages like `/sweaters`, `/raincoats`, and `/breed-fit` tend to show strong engagement.
- Blog content (dog fashion tips, seasonal care guides) drives organic search traffic but converts at a lower rate than direct product pages.
- The `/size-guide` page is a strong purchase-intent signal — visitors who view it convert at a much higher rate.
- Utility pages like `/account`, `/cart`, `/order-tracking`, and `/returns` should be treated cautiously in engagement analysis.
